| 1 | Título | 6 | Some recipes were never written. |
| 2 | Un puñito | 9 | "Le echas un puñito." A handful. No one ever measured it. And when that person is gone, so is the recipe. |
| 3 | Producto | 8 | Handful Recipes keeps their way of cooking, and the voice that makes it theirs. |
| 4 | Grabar | 10 | You just film them cooking. No script, no measuring. For this demo, we used a short test recording: a bowl of cereal. |
| 5 | Modelos | 13 | Then Meta's models get to work. Muse Voice Transcribe captures who said what. Muse Spark reads the video and the speech together. And SAM finds the hand or the container in each step. |
| 6 | Receta viva | 18 | The result is a living recipe, where every step keeps its source. Tap a step, and you're back at that moment in the video. If they poured it by eye, the recipe says "by eye." It shows the frame, and it never makes up a number. |
| 7 | Preguntas | 15 | Here's what makes it different. It notices what was left unsaid. How much milk? Which cereal? Served cold, or warm? Instead of guessing, it turns each gap into a question. |
| 8 | Respuesta | 14 | The cook gets a link. No app, no account. They answer with their voice, and the answer lands on the right step, in their own words. In this test, we used a synthetic voice. |
| 9 | Resultados | 14 | On this one test recording, processing took seventy-one seconds, and it invented no measurements. This isn't a benchmark. Accuracy still has to be measured, and SAM runs on still frames today. |
