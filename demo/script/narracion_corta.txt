Some recipes were never written.

"Le echas un puñito." A handful. No one ever measured it. And when that person is gone, so is the recipe.

Handful Recipes keeps their way of cooking. Just film them, no script, no measuring.

Meta's models listen and watch. Muse Voice Transcribe, Muse Spark and SAM turn the video into a living recipe.

Every step keeps its source, and it never makes up a number.

It notices what was left unsaid, and turns each gap into a question.

The cook gets a link, and answers with their voice.

We tested one short recording. It's not a benchmark, and accuracy still has to be measured.

All of this was built during the hackathon, because some recipes only live in someone's hands.
